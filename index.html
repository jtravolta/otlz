Sales sequence

From observation to deposit

  1. Find a good-fit business. Established US contractor or home improvement company with an improvable enquiry experience.
  2. Identify a specific issue. E.g. missing request-estimate button, a complicated contact form, or unclear next steps.
  3. Ask permission. Offer to send 2–3 practical suggestions.
  4. Provide a useful audit. State what's wrong, why it may matter, and your recommendation. Don't invent conversion data.
  5. Qualify. Ask how they get leads, what happens next, current pain, project economics, and deadline.
  6. Propose. Send a short fixed-scope offer with deliverables, timeline, exclusions, price, and terms.
  7. Close safely. Agree in writing and collect the deposit before beginning substantial work.

Five discovery questions

  1. How do most customers find you today?
  2. What happens after someone requests an estimate?
  3. Roughly how many website enquiries do you receive monthly?
  4. What's an average new project worth?
  5. What part of the enquiry process would you most like to improve?

Quick audit template

1. Observation: What I saw on your website.
2. Risk: How this may make enquiries harder.
3. Fix: The smallest useful improvement.
4. Next step: Invite a 10-minute conversation.
